# PB-ANCHOR-003 奇幻小龙角色锚点

- case_id: PB-ANCHOR-003
- use_case: 为轻奇幻冒险绘本建立小龙主角锚点。
- tags: 角色锚点, 成长冒险, 6-10岁, 轻奇幻

## prompt
Create the reusable main character for a light fantasy adventure children’s book.

Character:
A small dragon child named Luma, about the height of a human 8-year-old, round snout, big curious eyes, three tiny horns, short wings that cannot yet lift her, soft mint-green scales with pale yellow belly.
She wears a simple red scarf and carries a little star-shaped compass.

Purpose:
This dragon will appear across a 12-page light fantasy adventure story, so the design must stay stable and easily recognizable.

Style:
Whimsical children’s book illustration, soft shapes, gentle fantasy tone, friendly proportions, no sharp scary details, suitable for ages 6-10.

Composition:
Full body character, standing on a small hill, simple sky gradient behind, enough empty space to reuse the image as a reference.

Constraints:
Do not make the dragon look scary.
No realistic sharp teeth, no fire breathing.
Keep the scarf and compass as identity anchors.
No watermark.

## why_it_works
- 奇幻题材经常把龙画得太恐怖，提前限制 teeth 和 fire breathing 很关键。
- scarf 和 compass 作为锚点道具便于跨页维持角色识别度。

## replaceable_variables
- dragon child -> cloud fox / young phoenix / robot child
- scarf -> backpack / hat / necklace

## fix_lines
- Keep the dragon friendly and non-scary.
- Preserve the same scarf, compass, and proportions on every page.

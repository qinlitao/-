# PB-COVER-001 睡前森林封面

- case_id: PB-COVER-001
- use_case: 睡前题材绘本封面。
- tags: 封面, 睡前故事, 水彩, 夜晚, 4-8岁

## prompt
Create a premium children’s picture book cover for ages 4-8.

Book concept:
A shy little hedgehog girl learns to cross a glowing rainy forest path and discovers courage.

Main character:
Use the same hedgehog girl: round face, warm brown eyes, short chestnut spines, cream muzzle, mustard-yellow raincoat, teal rain boots, acorn-shaped satchel.
Do not redesign her.

Cover scene:
Twilight forest after rain, glowing puddles, tiny lantern bugs in the air, a curved path leading into the woods, the little hedgehog standing in the foreground looking both nervous and hopeful.

Style:
Beautiful children’s book cover illustration, watercolor and gouache, soft edges, luminous but gentle lighting, warm and safe emotional tone, bookstore-quality composition.

Composition:
Vertical portrait cover, title area reserved in the upper third, character in the lower-middle foreground, clear focal point, cinematic depth but simple readable shapes.

Constraints:
Keep the mood magical but comforting, not dark or scary.
No extra characters unless small distant forest animals are used as subtle secondary elements.
No watermark, no text rendering.

## why_it_works
- 明确 cover 用途和 title area，适合封面实际生产。
- 把“magical but comforting”说清楚，能减少夜景变恐怖的问题。

## replaceable_variables
- rainy forest -> snowy town / lantern street / moon base
- shy little hedgehog girl -> any anchored main character
- discovers courage -> learns patience / kindness / curiosity

## fix_lines
- Keep the focal point on the child, not the background decorations.
- Make the scene gentle, warm, and emotionally safe.

# PB-COVER-002 节日灯会封面

- case_id: PB-COVER-002
- use_case: 节日主题绘本封面。
- tags: 封面, 节日主题, 灯会, 温暖, 4-8岁

## prompt
Create a festive children’s picture book cover for ages 4-8.

Book concept:
A small animal child prepares a handmade lantern and joins the evening festival with family.

Main character:
Use the same anchored child character with the same outfit, face shape, and proportions.

Cover scene:
A lantern festival at dusk, warm paper lanterns overhead, glowing reflections on the street, the child holding a handmade lantern proudly in the foreground, family softly visible behind.

Style:
Storybook watercolor, warm festive glow, rich but controlled decorative detail, child-friendly celebration.

Composition:
Vertical cover, title area in upper third, clear central child focus, decorations framing rather than overpowering the subject.

Constraints:
Do not let the decorations overwhelm the main character.
Avoid chaotic crowd scenes.
No watermark, no text rendering.

## why_it_works
- 节日封面最容易失控在装饰上，所以直接写 framing rather than overpowering。
- family softly visible behind 可以强化陪伴感，又不抢主角。

## replaceable_variables
- lantern festival -> Christmas market / Spring Festival / birthday fair
- handmade lantern -> gift / kite / flower crown

## fix_lines
- Reduce crowd density and simplify background details.
- Keep the child as the visual center.

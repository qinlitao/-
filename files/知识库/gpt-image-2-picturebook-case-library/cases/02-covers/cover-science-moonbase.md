# PB-COVER-003 月球基地科普封面

- case_id: PB-COVER-003
- use_case: 科普 + 冒险题材的月球基地绘本封面。
- tags: 封面, 科普启蒙, 月球基地, 7-11岁

## prompt
Create an educational yet adventurous children’s picture book cover for ages 7-11.

Book concept:
A curious child spends one day exploring a moon base and learns how people live and work there.

Main character:
Use the anchored science child character: same face, same proportions, simple space suit with a colored stripe, clear helmet.

Cover scene:
Exterior view of the moon base under a starry sky, Earth visible in the distance, the child standing in the foreground on the gray lunar surface, pointing toward the base with excitement.

Style:
Bright educational picture book art, clean shapes, subtle sci-fi details, not realistic hard science fiction, friendly and inspiring.

Composition:
Horizontal or vertical cover layout with a clear title area, base buildings on one side, child on the other, Earth high in the sky as a visual anchor.

Constraints:
No dark horror space mood.
No overly technical realism.
No cluttered UI screens.
No watermark, no text rendering.

## why_it_works
- 把 educational yet adventurous 写在一起，能兼顾知识感与故事感。
- Earth 作为视觉锚点有助于读者一眼认出“这是月球”。

## replaceable_variables
- moon base -> underwater lab / rainforest station / desert observatory
- space suit -> exploration jacket / lab coat

## fix_lines
- Keep the cover bright, inspiring, and child-friendly.
- Avoid dark or frightening space imagery.

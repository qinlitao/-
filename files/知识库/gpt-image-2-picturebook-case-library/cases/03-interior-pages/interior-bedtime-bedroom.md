# PB-INNER-006 睡前卧室安抚页

- case_id: PB-INNER-006
- use_case: 睡前故事中，主角被安抚准备入睡的页面。
- tags: 内页, 睡前故事, 卧室, 安抚, 3-7岁

## prompt
Create a bedtime picture book illustration for ages 3-7.

Scene:
The child protagonist sits on the edge of a cozy bed, a parent or caregiver gently tucks in the blanket.
A small night-light glows softly on a bedside table, with one favorite toy nearby.

Style:
Warm watercolor or soft pastel, low contrast, gentle edges, very calm mood, safe and comforting.

Composition:
Medium-wide bedroom interior, bed and characters clearly visible, window with simple night sky outside, enough negative space around the bed for text.

Constraints:
Keep the lighting dim but not dark.
No harsh shadows, no scary shapes.
No cluttered background.
No watermark, no text.

## why_it_works
- 睡前页的核心是光线和表情，dim but not dark 能避免画面变压抑。
- caregiver 动作比语言更关键，所以强调 tucks in the blanket。

## replaceable_variables
- parent or caregiver -> grandparent / older sibling
- night-light -> small lamp / string lights

## fix_lines
- Make the bedroom feel safe, calm, and gently lit.
- Remove any shapes that could be misread as scary.

# PB-INNER-004 节日灯会内页

- case_id: PB-INNER-004
- use_case: 节日主题绘本的庆典内页。
- tags: 内页, 节日主题, 灯会, 温暖, 4-8岁

## prompt
Create a festive children’s picture book illustration for ages 4-8.

Scene:
The same hedgehog girl joins a lantern festival at dusk with her family.
Warm paper lanterns hang above a small town street, but the main focus stays on the child holding a handmade lantern with pride.

Character consistency:
Same hedgehog girl, same face, same body proportions, same mustard-yellow raincoat, same teal rain boots.

Style:
Storybook watercolor, warm festive glow, soft layered lights, child-friendly celebration, rich but controlled details.

Composition:
Vertical scene, child in foreground, family slightly behind, lanterns framing the upper area, clear path leading into the scene.

Constraints:
Do not let decorations overwhelm the character.
Keep the palette warm and harmonious.
No chaotic crowd.
No realistic adult fashion styling.
No watermark.

## why_it_works
- 对节日页来说，rich but controlled details 是非常实用的控制语。
- 明确 child in foreground 可减少画面变成“背景灯饰图”。

## replaceable_variables
- lantern festival -> spring fair / winter market / mooncake night
- family -> classmates / neighbors / grandparents

## fix_lines
- Simplify the crowd and preserve a clear focal point.
- Keep the lanterns secondary to the child.

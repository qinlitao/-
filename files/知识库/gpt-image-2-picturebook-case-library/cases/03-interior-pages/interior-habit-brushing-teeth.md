# PB-INNER-007 习惯养成：刷牙页

- case_id: PB-INNER-007
- use_case: 习惯养成绘本中关于刷牙的教学页面。
- tags: 内页, 习惯养成, 刷牙, 日常, 3-8岁

## prompt
Create a children’s picture book illustration teaching tooth-brushing for ages 3-8.

Scene:
A small child stands on a sturdy stool at the bathroom sink, brushing teeth in front of a mirror.
A friendly animal-shaped timer and a simple tooth-brushing chart are visible nearby.

Style:
Bright, clean, cheerful picture book art, simple bathroom layout, hygienic and fresh feeling.

Composition:
Medium shot, child and sink centered, mirror reflecting a clear happy face with foamy toothpaste, simple tiled background.

Constraints:
Avoid medical or clinical mood.
No scary dentist tools.
No clutter of many bottles.
No watermark, no text.

## why_it_works
- 习惯类页面要让行为看起来简单可模仿，因此 stool + mirror + chart 的三点组合很实用。

## replaceable_variables
- tooth-brushing chart -> sticker chart / star board
- timer -> sand timer / little alarm clock

## fix_lines
- Keep the bathroom simple, bright, and friendly.
- Make the tooth-brushing action easy to understand.

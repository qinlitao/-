# PB-INNER-009 自然观察：放大镜页

- case_id: PB-INNER-009
- use_case: 自然观察或科普绘本中，小读者观察细节的页面。
- tags: 内页, 自然观察, 放大镜, 6-10岁

## prompt
Create a nature observation picture book illustration for ages 6-10.

Scene:
The child kneels in a meadow, using a magnifying glass to look closely at a line of ants carrying leaves.
Nearby flowers and grass create a simple frame around the focus area.

Style:
Detailed but not cluttered children’s book art, natural greens and browns, soft sunlight, educational but playful.

Composition:
Medium-close shot to clearly show the ants and magnifying glass, child on one side, ants on the other, blurred background beyond the meadow.

Constraints:
Do not turn the ants into scary monsters.
Keep the environment realistic but simplified.
No watermark, no text.

## why_it_works
- 把 focus 放在 ants + magnifying glass，既有知识点又有故事感。

## replaceable_variables
- ants -> beetles / snails / small flowers
- meadow -> garden / park corner

## fix_lines
- Emphasize curiosity and small details.
- Keep the insects friendly and non-threatening.

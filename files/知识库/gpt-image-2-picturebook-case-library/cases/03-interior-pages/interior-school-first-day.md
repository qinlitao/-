# PB-INNER-008 校园日常：第一天上学页

- case_id: PB-INNER-008
- use_case: 校园日常或成长冒险的“第一天上学”情节页。
- tags: 内页, 校园日常, 第一日, 4-8岁

## prompt
Create a children’s picture book scene of a child’s first day of school for ages 4-8.

Scene:
The child stands at the school gate with a backpack, looking both nervous and excited.
Other children walk in small groups in the background, a friendly teacher waves from near the door.

Style:
Warm, optimistic storybook illustration, clear shapes, not overcrowded, gentle morning light.

Composition:
Wide shot of the school entrance, child slightly in the foreground, gate and school building visible, banner or sign with a simple school symbol (no text).

Constraints:
Avoid chaotic crowds.
No bullying or harsh expressions.
No detailed text on signs.
No watermark.

## why_it_works
- 第一日上学页的关键是 gate + backpack + mixed emotions，这里全部点明。

## replaceable_variables
- school gate -> classroom door / bus stop
- friendly teacher -> principal / older student helper

## fix_lines
- Keep the scene welcoming and not overwhelming.
- Show mixed feelings but end in a hopeful tone.

# PB-INNER-003 科普发芽页

- case_id: PB-INNER-003
- use_case: 科普启蒙绘本中的结构解释页。
- tags: 内页, 科普启蒙, 发芽, 图解, 7-10岁

## prompt
Create a science picture book illustration for ages 7-10 explaining how a seed grows.

Scene:
A cross-section garden bed showing one bean seed underground beginning to sprout.
Above ground, the young hedgehog girl watches with curiosity and holds a tiny notebook.

Educational goal:
The image should clearly show seed coat, root, first sprout, soil layers, and sunlight direction in a child-friendly way.

Style:
Bright educational picture book art, clean shapes, watercolor plus simple infographic clarity, natural colors, easy-to-read visual hierarchy.

Composition:
Landscape educational spread, underground section visible in the lower half, above-ground garden in the upper half, central sprouting seed as the main focus.

Constraints:
Make the biology visually understandable for children.
Avoid dense labels inside the image.
Avoid visual clutter.
No watermark, no unnecessary decorative elements.

## why_it_works
- 教育目标被单独列出，能显著提高知识点呈现的稳定性。
- cross-section + upper/lower half 让结构页更容易生成清晰布局。

## replaceable_variables
- seed grows -> tadpole changes / moon phases / ant nest / volcano layers
- hedgehog girl -> science anchor character

## fix_lines
- Prioritize educational clarity over decoration.
- Simplify the layout and keep the biology readable.

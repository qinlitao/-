# PB-INNER-002 社交情绪修复页

- case_id: PB-INNER-002
- use_case: 社交情绪绘本中的误会修复场景。
- tags: 内页, 社交情绪, 友情修复, 教室, 5-8岁

## prompt
Create a children’s picture book illustration about friendship repair for ages 5-8.

Scene:
Two small animal friends have just had a misunderstanding while building a paper boat.
One rabbit child looks upset but not angry.
The hedgehog girl gently offers half of the folded paper and looks apologetic.

Main character:
Use the same hedgehog girl with mustard-yellow raincoat, teal rain boots, and acorn satchel nearby.

Style:
Warm watercolor children’s book illustration, expressive faces, clear body language, soft classroom window light, calm emotional storytelling.

Composition:
Medium-wide interior scene, both characters fully visible, table between them, torn paper boat pieces visible, enough empty space around them for future text placement.

Constraints:
Focus on facial expression, posture, and emotional clarity.
Do not make the scene melodramatic.
No shouting, no aggression, no adult facial styling, no cluttered classroom background.

## why_it_works
- 社交情绪页的关键不是复杂背景，而是表情、姿态和互动距离。
- upset but not angry 这类限制语能防止模型把儿童冲突画得太激烈。

## replaceable_variables
- paper boat -> block tower / kite / drawing book
- classroom -> playground / living room / art room

## fix_lines
- Make the emotions clear but gentle.
- Reduce drama and keep the body language readable.

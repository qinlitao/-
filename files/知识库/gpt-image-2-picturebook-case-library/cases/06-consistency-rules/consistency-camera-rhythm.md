# PB-RULE-002 镜头节奏规则

- case_id: PB-RULE-002
- use_case: 给整本绘本安排镜头变化，避免每页都一样。
- tags: 一致性规则, 镜头节奏, 分镜

## prompt
Apply picture-book camera rhythm across the full story.
Alternate wide shot, medium shot, and close-up when appropriate.
Use wider shots for scene setting, medium shots for interaction, and close-ups for emotional moments.
Do not repeat the same framing on too many consecutive pages.
Keep the visual storytelling simple and readable for children.

## why_it_works
- 对多页绘本来说，这段规则能明显改善视觉节奏。
- 它不是指定每页镜头，而是提供稳定但可适应剧情的调度逻辑。

## replaceable_variables
- simple and readable for children -> more exploratory for 9-12
- wide/medium/close-up ratio

## fix_lines
- Vary the framing from page to page.
- Keep the sequence readable and child-friendly.

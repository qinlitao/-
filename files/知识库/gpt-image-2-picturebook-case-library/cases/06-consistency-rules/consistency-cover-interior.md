# PB-RULE-003 封面与内页统一规则

- case_id: PB-RULE-003
- use_case: 保持封面、正文、封底属于同一本书。
- tags: 一致性规则, 封面内页统一, 系列感

## prompt
Keep full-book visual consistency.
Use the same palette family, brush softness, lighting logic, character design, and emotional tone on the cover, interior pages, and back cover.
The cover may be slightly richer and more dramatic, but it must still clearly belong to the same book world.
Do not let the interior pages shift into a different rendering style.

## why_it_works
- 很多项目失败在封面好看、内页变样，这段规则就是专门补这个问题。
- cover may be slightly richer 给封面留了发挥空间，但又防止完全换风格。

## replaceable_variables
- palette family
- lighting logic
- emotional tone

## fix_lines
- Match the same world, palette, and brush texture as the cover.
- Do not switch rendering style between pages.
